## 0. NORTH STAR

MARKET.INK exists to become the specialist growth operating system for tattoo studios and tattoo artists.

We do not sell "social media management" as the product.

We help a studio turn artistic quality, reputation and available capacity into a repeatable operating system:

```text
POSITION
→ OFFER
→ CONTENT
→ DISCOVERY
→ CONVERSATION
→ QUALIFICATION
→ BOOKING
→ DEPOSIT
→ APPOINTMENT
→ REVIEW
→ REBOOKING
→ REFERRAL
→ DATA
→ OPTIMIZATION
```

The business has three layers that deliberately reinforce each other:

1. **LEARN — MARKET.INK Academy / INK GROWTH OS**
   - teach studio owners and artists to build the system themselves;
   - provide templates, checklists, automations, dashboards and operating routines.

2. **IMPLEMENT / DONE-FOR-YOU — MARKET.INK Growth**
   - implement and operate the same system for clients;
   - content, social, Meta Ads, web, CRM, automation, lead handling, booking and reporting.

3. **SOFTWARE — InkFlow / tattoo-saas**
   - once the studio has demand, discipline and operational maturity, migrate repetitive workflows into dedicated software;
   - software is an expansion layer, not the entry offer.

The client-facing promise is business growth and professionalization, not "AI".

### North Star Metric

`PROFITABLE_BOOKED_CAPACITY_RATE`

```text
confirmed economically viable tattoo hours
-------------------------------------------
sellable tattoo hours
```

Supporting client metrics:

- cost per deposit;

- repeat booking rate;

Followers, views, likes and reach are diagnostic metrics, not the outcome.

# 1. CUSTOMER STRATEGY — OWNER FIRST, ARTIST SECOND

## Primary ICP — Studio owner / manager

The default sale is top-down.

Why:
- one owner controls the operating environment;
- one sale can activate multiple artists;
- a studio has more total capacity;
- centralized CRM, content, ads, reviews and reporting create more leverage;
- case studies become richer because demand can be routed by style and artist.

Primary motion:

```text
STUDIO OWNER
→ INKSCAN
→ VSL / STRATEGY CALL
→ ACADEMY or DFY
→ ENTIRE ARTIST TEAM
→ 90-DAY GROWTH SYSTEM
→ RETAINER
→ INKFLOW
```

## Secondary ICP — Individual artist

If the owner does not buy, pursue high-fit individual artists.

Bottom-up motion:

```text
ARTIST
→ RESULT / PROOF
→ INTERNAL WORD OF MOUTH
→ PEERS
→ OWNER
→ STUDIO-WIDE UPGRADE
```

This is intentional land-and-expand.

An individual artist is not a failed studio sale; they can become the internal champion.

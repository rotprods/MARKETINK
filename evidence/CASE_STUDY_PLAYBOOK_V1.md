# MARKET.INK — Case Study Evidence Playbook V1

The website, VSL, ads and sales material may only use proof that passes this system.

## 1. REQUIRED CHAIN

```text
BASELINE
→ INTERVENTION
→ OBSERVATION PERIOD
→ VERIFIED RESULT
→ ATTRIBUTION ASSESSMENT
→ CLIENT PERMISSION
→ CLAIM APPROVAL
```

Missing one link means the result is not ready for public use.

---

## 2. ATTRIBUTION LEVELS

### DIRECT
Event/revenue can be tied through a defensible data chain.

Example:
campaign/ad → lead → deposit → booking → booked value.

### STRONG
Multiple systems agree, with minor gaps.

### PARTIAL
Some linkage exists but not all steps.

### CORRELATIONAL
Metric changed during intervention period but causality cannot be isolated.

### UNKNOWN
Do not imply causation.

---

## 3. CLAIM EXAMPLES

If evidence is DIRECT:

Allowed:
> “During the measured period, campaigns generated X tracked deposits at Y tracked cost per deposit.”

Still avoid:
> “MARKET.INK will do the same for you.”

If evidence is CORRELATIONAL:

Allowed:
> “Booked capacity increased from X to Y during the intervention period.”

Required caveat:
> “Other factors may also have contributed.”

Not allowed:
> “Our ads caused the entire increase.”

---

## 4. BEFORE / AFTER

Never choose misleading windows.

Record:
- same duration where possible;
- seasonality;
- promotions;
- artist absence;
- price changes;
- capacity changes;
- major external events.

---

## 5. TESTIMONIALS

Need:
- exact approved wording or approved recording;
- speaker identity/role if public;
- public-use permission;
- no editing that changes meaning.

Do not turn a casual WhatsApp compliment into an ad without permission.

---

## 6. SCREENSHOTS

Before public use:
- redact customer PII;
- redact phone/email;
- redact payment data;
- remove private messages unless explicitly permitted;
- preserve enough context to avoid misleading interpretation.

---

## 7. CASE STUDY STRUCTURE

1. Client context
2. Baseline
3. Constraint
4. Intervention
5. What MARKET.INK controlled
6. What client controlled
7. Result
8. Measurement method
9. Limitations
10. Client quote
11. Next experiment

---

## 8. WEBSITE GATE

A case may render on the public website only if:

- status = APPROVED;
- permission.public_use = true;
- at least one result has verified = true;
- evidence refs exist;
- claim wording is listed under claims_allowed;
- no forbidden claim is present.

---

## 9. VSL GATE

Any numerical proof in VSL additionally requires:
- on-screen timeframe;
- metric definition;
- no tiny-font caveat trick;
- spoken wording matches evidence strength.

---

## 10. RETEST / EXPIRY

Review public cases when:
- metric definition changed;
- attribution system changed;
- client revoked permission;
- evidence source became unavailable;
- case is old enough that wording could imply current performance incorrectly.

Use status:
`EXPIRED`

Do not silently keep stale proof live.

# MARKET.INK — Founding Beta Operator Program V1

> **Purpose:** validate the Academy + implementation system with 3–5 real tattoo operators before public launch.  
> **Primary persona:** studio owner / manager.  
> **Secondary persona:** high-fit independent artist.  
> **Duration hypothesis:** 6 weeks.  
> **Status:** recruitment-ready, pricing still experimental.

---

# 0. WHY A BETA EXISTS

The beta is not a discount launch.

It is a controlled product-development program.

MARKET.INK needs to learn:
- what operators can implement alone;
- where instructions fail;
- which templates save time;
- which steps require service support;
- what data studios actually have;
- where agency delivery creates the most value;
- which repeated pain belongs in InkFlow.

Success is not:
“they liked the course.”

Success is:
“they produced working business systems and we can prove which parts helped.”

---

# 1. COHORT DESIGN

Target:
**3–5 operators**

Recommended mix:
- 2 multi-artist studio owners;
- 1 small studio owner / manager;
- 1 independent artist;
- optional 1 high-potential operator from an adjacent Murcia-area market.

Do not recruit five identical businesses.

We want variance in:
- team size;
- booking maturity;
- content maturity;
- Instagram/WhatsApp behavior;
- data quality;
- available capacity.

---

# 2. ENTRY GATE

## Studio

Minimum:
- active commercial studio;
- owner/manager participates;
- 2+ active artists preferred;
- professional Instagram or willingness to configure it;
- WhatsApp/DM inbound exists;
- can estimate monthly capacity;
- willing to share non-sensitive operating baseline;
- can implement 2–3 hours/week;
- accepts measurement.

## Independent artist

Minimum:
- commercially active;
- portfolio has clear real client work;
- some monthly inquiry volume;
- enough open capacity to test improvement;
- willing to implement and measure.

---

# 3. DISQUALIFIERS

Do not recruit when:
- no real commercial activity;
- no capacity and no strategic waitlist goal;
- only interested in “getting followers”;
- refuses to measure anything;
- cannot commit implementation time;
- expects guaranteed revenue;
- wants deceptive marketing;
- unresolved serious safety/compliance concern;
- joins only because it is free.

---

# 4. BETA OFFER

Participants receive:

1. Foundation Track M00–M05 pre-release.
2. A01–A06 implementation assets.
3. Initial INKSCAN / operating baseline.
4. Weekly implementation checkpoint.
5. Artifact QA.
6. Private defect/support channel.
7. Priority access to subsequent Content/Demand modules as they stabilize.
8. Closing INKSCAN / retrospective.
9. Optional DFY migration plan.
10. Optional InkFlow readiness assessment.

Not included by default:
- unlimited consulting;
- full monthly content production;
- unlimited ad management;
- custom software development.

Those belong to paid services.

# 5. COMMERCIAL EXPERIMENT

Pricing is a hypothesis, not yet MARKET.INK canon.

## Test A — Studio
`€397 founding commitment`

Up to 3 active artists included for beta learning context.

## Test B — Independent
`€197 founding commitment`

## Credit mechanism

100% may be credited toward a qualifying MARKET.INK Launch engagement started within 30 days after beta completion.

Why:
- filters low-intent freebie behavior;
- creates real purchase signal;
- keeps beta economically accessible;
- preserves upgrade continuity.

## Strategic exception

Maximum:
**1 free design partner**

Only when the studio has unusually high learning/case-study value.

Free access cannot require a positive testimonial.

# 8. WEEKLY CHECKPOINT FORMAT

Maximum default:
30 minutes.

Agenda:

```text
5m  — what was implemented
5m  — evidence/results
10m — blockers
5m  — one correction
5m  — exact next action
```

Do not turn beta into open-ended consulting.

---

# 10. PASS / FAIL

A beta operator passes when:
- baseline exists;
- M00–M05 artifacts exist;
- one activation experiment runs;
- final retrospective exists.

MARKET.INK beta succeeds when:
- >= 70% invited/paid participants complete;
- all P0/P1 curriculum defects are repaired;
- median support burden is understood;
- at least 3 operators produce complete artifact sets;
- we know which modules drive DIY vs DFY demand;
- proof claims remain evidence-safe.

These are beta learning gates, not marketing claims.

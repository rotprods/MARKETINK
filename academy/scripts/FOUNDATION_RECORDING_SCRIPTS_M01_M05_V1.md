# M02 — OFFER ARCHITECTURE

## L01 — Offer is not discount

Open with two examples:
- "20% off all tattoos"
- "Fine Line Project Week — 8 project slots with Mara"

Explain difference:
one destroys price as the only reason; the other creates structured relevance/capacity.

---

## L02 — 20 offer types

Move quickly through canonical categories.

Do not make learner memorize them.

Goal:
show possibility space.

---

## L03 — Economics before publishing

Use Mara:
44 open hours.

Ask:
what offer should fill that capacity without displacing Nico's premium realism work?

Student records:
- artist;
- capacity;
- minimum acceptable project;
- time window.

## L04 — Weekly cadence

campaign rhythm > random "we have availability" Stories.

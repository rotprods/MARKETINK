## M00-L01 — THE GROWTH EQUATION

### Hook

"Antes de publicar más, gastar en anuncios o contratar una agencia, necesitamos responder una pregunta: ¿qué parte de tu negocio está limitando realmente el crecimiento?"

### Show

Display:

```text
CAPACITY × DEMAND × CONVERSION × VALUE × RETENTION
```

Explain:
- zero capacity → more leads can create a worse customer experience;
- enough demand + weak conversion → ads amplify leakage;
- strong conversion + empty calendar → acquisition becomes logical.

### Action

Student writes their suspected current bottleneck.

### End card

`OUTPUT: suspected constraint`

## M00-L03 — TICKET & ECONOMICS

### Demo

Show average ticket €338 and deposit €60.

Explain:

- do not use industry averages as if they were your actual economics;
- unknown = unknown.

Enter actual values or mark UNKNOWN.

## M00-L05 — ACQUISITION CEILING & ATTRIBUTION

### Demo

Meta:
€540 / 46 inquiries = €11.74 per inquiry.

Pause.

Explain explicitly:

> "Esto no es CAC y no es ROAS."

Why:
source does not follow through to deposit/booking/revenue.

### Student action

Mark:
- what can be verified;
- what cannot;
- what must begin tracking.

## M00-L06 — FIND THE BOTTLENECK

### Demo

Ask:
- demand?
- conversion?
- capacity routing?
- attribution?

Conclude for synthetic studio:
conversion + artist routing first, attribution second.

### Show next actions

1. repair stages;
2. route fine-line demand;
3. create a Mara-specific offer;
4. connect campaign/creative IDs;
5. only then decide whether to scale spend.

### Student action

Write one primary constraint and three next actions.
